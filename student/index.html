Robert Koirala

Student

This project was inspired by a saying in sanskrit below that describes the five qualities of a student: the persistence of a crow, the concentration of a crane, the light sleep of a dog, moderation in food, and renunciation of home.

काकचेष्टा बको ध्यानं श्वाननिद्रा तथैव च।
अल्पाहारी गृहत्यागी विद्यार्थी पंचलक्षणम्॥
